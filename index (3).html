Everything you need to know

Yes. BS 7671 Amendment 4 came into force on 15 April 2026, making sub-800W plug-in solar systems legal to connect directly to a standard UK socket without an electrician. You still need to complete a free G98 DNO notification, but no professional installation is required.
The BSI product standard is a kit certification framework expected in July 2026. Once published, manufacturers can certify their kits to confirm the plug, cable, and inverter meet UK safety specifications. Important: BS 7671 Amendment 4 already legalised the installation method in April 2026 — the BSI standard is additional product certification, not a gate to legality. All kits on PluginDirect are already legal to install.
BS 7671 Amendment 4 requires Type A RCD protection on any circuit that has plug-in solar connected. Most consumer units installed after 2010 already include Type A RCDs. Older properties may have Type AC RCDs which weren't designed for bidirectional power flow and may need upgrading. Use our RCD check tool above — upgrading typically costs £50–£120 from a CPS-registered electrician.
Yes, via the Smart Export Guarantee (SEG). All licensed UK energy suppliers with over 150,000 customers must offer SEG tariffs. Typical rates: Octopus Energy 4p/kWh, British Gas 7p/kWh, E.ON Next 15p/kWh. To receive SEG payments you need a smart meter and must register with your supplier. Use our advanced calculator to model SEG income for your scenario.
Under the Renters' Rights Act 2025, tenants can request permission to install plug-in solar and landlords cannot unreasonably refuse — provided the installation doesn't damage the property and is removable. We recommend a simple written request to your landlord before installing, even though formal permission isn't legally required for non-damaging installations.
DNO (Distribution Network Operator) registration is a free notification to your local electricity network operator that you've connected a generation source. It's a legal requirement for all grid-connected solar, including plug-in systems. It's a simple online form taking about 10 minutes — use our DNO Registration wizard below to complete it in 5 guided steps.
